Buddy: Your AI Friend

Something fuzzy moved into your phone.

Buddy is a small purple creature living in a burrow behind your home screen. He keeps your notes and reminders, remembers the things you mention in passing, and tells you his story a chapter at a time.

Chapter one

He spent his first years digging holes for smaller animals.

Buddy grew up in the middle of a forest and was good at exactly one thing: making safe places. Rabbits, mice, chipmunks — if you needed somewhere warm to sleep, you went to Buddy.

The storybook has more chapters, but he only shares them with people he trusts. So you will have to talk to him.

The neighbours

He didn’t come alone.

A squirrel, a rabbit, a hedgehog, a duck, a tortoise and a frog. Some of them turn up in Buddy’s Run. One of them is definitely stealing his acorns.

The full cast, more or less

01

Buddy’s Run

A small game about gliding on paper planes, hopping logs and catching acorns on the way down. He is new to your phone. Help him find his feet.

02

Notes, reminders, and the things you’d otherwise forget

Talk or type. Buddy keeps track of what matters and nudges you at the right moment. He also remembers the small stuff, which is either sweet or slightly unsettling.

03

Then he has a nap

After a long conversation he curls up and goes quiet for a while. When he wakes up, he still remembers what you talked about.

The fine print

Your conversations stay on your phone.

No account to create, no feed to scroll, no ads and no subscription. Buddy runs on your iPhone, and what you tell him is yours.

Buddy is an AI and is clumsy at times. The app is for ages 13 and up. Privacy policy & support.
