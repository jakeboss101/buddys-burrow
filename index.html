His story starts in the forest

Buddy grew up in a forest, digging burrows so the little animals had somewhere safe. 🐾

That's only Chapter 1. Talk with him, become friends, and he'll share the rest of his storybook, one chapter at a time.

His little friends

A squirrel, a bunny, a hedgehog, a duck, a turtle and a frog. You'll meet some of them in Buddy's Run.

Things you can do together

Play Buddy's Run

Help him glide, jump and catch acorns while he settles into your phone.

Chat, notes and reminders

Talk or type. He remembers what matters and reminds you when it's time.

Tuck him in

After a long chat he takes a quick nap, and wakes up remembering what you talked about.

Your chats stay yours

Buddy runs on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no subscription.
